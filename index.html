Animal signals convey meaning

What if we could understand them? The sounds of nature contain boundless untapped information.

02 / 05

Can we interpret these signals?

Diana monkeys emit specific alarm calls for specific predators. Other species — and now humans — heed the warning.

03 / 05

Can these signals inform our actions?

Animals detected the 2004 tsunami hours before it hit. Early detection → early intervention.

04 / 05

Can we help them by better listening?

Pets hide pain. Wildlife suffer silently. If we understand distress signals at scale, we can direct resources to intervene.

05 / 05

When we connect, we act

The discovery of humpback song helped end commercial whaling. Understanding drives compassionate action.

The Challenge

We are only beginning to understand animal signals

Only a small fraction of the species that communicate have been studied. Technology is advancing faster than the research necessary to guide it.

The Opportunity

Biology, data science, creative outreach, and ecology

By integrating global datasets and advanced data science, we can transform how we listen to animal signals — grounding our work in what matters to welfare and partnering with the public in discovery.
